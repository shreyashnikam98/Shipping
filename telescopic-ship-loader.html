EQUIPMENT OVERVIEW

Telescopic Ship Loader

An adjustable boom arrangement to help reach different loading positions. The final arrangement can be considered around site conditions, material characteristics, vessel geometry and the required operating sequence.

Key considerations

  • Integration with upstream material receiving
  • Suitable conveying and discharge arrangement
  • Operational access and positioning requirements
  • Maintenance access and safe operation
  • Coordination with the overall cargo handling layout
