SYSTEM OVERVIEW

From receiving point to vessel hold

A ship loading system combines equipment and operating steps into a coordinated cargo flow.

01

Material receiving

Bulk cargo is delivered to the designated receiving area.

02

Hopper feeding

A hopper receives and regulates the feed into the conveying arrangement.

03

Conveyor transportation

Belt conveyors transfer material towards the ship loading position.

04

Ship loader positioning

The loading equipment is positioned to suit the vessel and berth arrangement.

05

Controlled discharge

Material is discharged into the vessel cargo hold according to the operating plan.

APPLICATIONS

Bulk and bagged cargo handling

System design and equipment selection should reflect the cargo form and handling method.

Bulk cargo

Coal, iron ore, fertiliser, grain, minerals and other suitable bulk materials.

Bagged cargo

Loading arrangements for bagged products can be evaluated according to package, transfer and vessel requirements.

Vessel operations

Loading arrangements for ships, vessels and barges subject to berth and access conditions.
